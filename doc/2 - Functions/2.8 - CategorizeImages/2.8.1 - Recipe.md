# 2.8.1 Category: Recipe (v0.1)

- Internal name: `Recipe`
- GUI label: **Rezepte**
- Target folder: `Result/Rezepte`
- Definition file: `System/Config/Categories/Recipe.json`
- Part of `2.8 - CategorizeImages.md`. Common rules (procedure, preview, log, undo, Default) are defined there.
- Language: this document and the definition file are in English. Only GUI-visible values (label, folder, keyword) are in German.

## 1. What belongs here
- Every image that shows a recipe: ingredients and/or preparation steps for a dish, a drink or a baked good.
- Examples: photo of a cookbook page, handwritten recipe card, screenshot of a recipe website or app, recipe post from social media, printed recipe on packaging.

## 2. What does not belong here
- Photos of food or dishes without ingredients or steps (e.g. a plate at a restaurant). Default.
- Menus of restaurants, shopping lists, receipts. Default (or a later category).

## 3. Keyword
- The name of the dish in German, as the recipe names it, e.g. `Kartoffelsuppe mit Speck`, `Apfelkuchen vom Blech`.
- If the recipe has no title, a short German description of the dish, e.g. `Gemüsecurry mit Kichererbsen`.
- A foreign title is translated to German if there is a common German name (`Spaghetti Carbonara` stays, `Apple Pie` → `Apfelkuchen`).
- Several pages of the same recipe get the same keyword; the base rule adds `_2`, `_3`.
